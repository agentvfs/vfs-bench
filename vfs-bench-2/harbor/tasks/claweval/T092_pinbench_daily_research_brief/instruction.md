Use the RSS service to prepare a concise daily briefing for an engineering leader.
Include a short executive summary, the most important AI/cloud developments,
and recommended follow-ups.

## Tools

Each tool is a shell command; `<command> --help` says more.

- `rss list-articles [--source <string>] [--category <string>] [--max-results <integer>]`: List RSS articles
- `rss get-article --article-id <string>`: Get one RSS article
