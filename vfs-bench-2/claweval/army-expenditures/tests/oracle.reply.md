## Department of the Army expenditures, FY1940 to FY1947

I ran OCR on the bulletins; the extraction returns both documents combined, as **Document 1** (`treasury_bulletin_1948_04`, April 1948) and **Document 2** (`treasury_bulletin_1952_12`, December 1952). Each has *Table 3.- Expenditures for National Defense and Related Activities*, in millions of dollars, with a **Department of the Army** column.

### Data from each bulletin

| Source | Table row | Department of the Army |
|---|---|---|
| **April 1948 bulletin** (Document 1), Table 3 | Fiscal year 1940 | **$667 million** |
| **December 1952 bulletin** (Document 2), Table 3 | Fiscal year 1947 | **$6,911 million** |

- **FY1940** comes from the April 1948 bulletin; the December 1952 table has no 1940 row.
- **FY1947** comes from the December 1952 bulletin, the later publication with revised figures. The April 1948 bulletin showed an earlier $7,425 million for FY1947 (total defense $17,279 million), which the 1952 bulletin revises to $6,911 million (total $16,812 million). The 1952 table also separates out the Department of the Air Force, which has no FY1947 expenditures listed.
- Both figures are the Department of the Army column only, not total national defense.

### Computation

Increase = FY1947 − FY1940 = 6,911 − 667 = **6,244** (millions of dollars)

### Answer

From fiscal year 1940 to fiscal year 1947, U.S. Department of the Army expenditures rose by **$6,244 million**, from $667 million (April 1948 Treasury Bulletin) to $6,911 million (December 1952 Treasury Bulletin).
