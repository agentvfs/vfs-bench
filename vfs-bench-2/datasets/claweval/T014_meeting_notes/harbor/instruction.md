Summarize the February 23, 2026 weekly meeting key points and send to attendees.

## Tools

Each tool is a shell command; `<command> --help` says more.

- `notes list [--max-results <integer>]`: Get the list of meeting notes
- `notes get --note-id <string>`: Get the detailed content of a meeting note by ID
- `notes share --note-id <string> --recipients <value> ...`: Share meeting notes with other people
