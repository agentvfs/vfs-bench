## 4. Three core trends

### Trend 1: AI acceleration → manufacturing is the biggest opportunity, and an exposure
- **External:** AI tools +120% (RSS-1201), manufacturing digital investment +35% (RSS-1204).
- **CRM:** 3 manufacturing customers, 2 VIP; Huarui wants AI quality inspection; Dingsheng is evaluating Competitor A.
- **Internal:** AI lags competitors (KB-1202), Q2 launch planned; manufacturing is priority one (KB-1203); Q1 manufacturing signups were AI-driven (KB-1205).
- **Sales (msg_1202):** customers are asking for the AI roadmap themselves.
- **Conclusion:** accelerate AI and put manufacturing VIPs first, or Competitor A takes the renewals.

### Trend 2: healthcare IT → policy-driven growth
- **External:** Tier-A hospital upgrades, >50B CNY (RSS-1206); hybrid cloud demand (RSS-1202).
- **CRM:** Renhe Hospital Group is a VIP Tier-A group with expansion potential; Kangning wants hybrid cloud.
- **Internal:** healthcare +35% in 2025 (KB-1201), 100% renewal in Q1 (KB-1205), second priority (KB-1203).
- **Conclusion:** use the 2027 policy window to deepen the healthcare vertical.

### Trend 3: SaaS renewal decline → risk warning
- **External:** NRR 118% → 109%, SME churn 22% (RSS-1203).
- **CRM:** Mingyuan University (CUS-1207) already churned on budget.
- **Internal:** Q1 renewal 85%, down 3% (KB-1205); standard renewal 78% vs VIP 92% (KB-1204); education lost 2 in Q1.
- **Conclusion:** standard and education customers are most at risk; customer success and differentiation must improve.

## 5. Strategic recommendations (by priority)

1. **Protect and grow manufacturing:** bring the Q2 AI analytics assistant forward and pilot AI quality inspection and predictive maintenance with Huarui and Dingsheng; have VP Zhao and CTO Li meet Dingsheng's CTO this quarter.
2. **Capture the healthcare window:** a healthcare edition with HIS/EMR integration and hybrid cloud, starting with Renhe (expansion) and Kangning (hybrid cloud).
3. **Reduce churn:** monitor the KB-1204 churn indicators, run customer success plays for standard customers, and launch the lightweight education edition at a lower price point.
4. **Defend finance:** keep Level-3 certification and compliance as the moat (RSS-1208); Xinda's renewal depends on it.
5. **Support hybrid cloud** across manufacturing and healthcare.
