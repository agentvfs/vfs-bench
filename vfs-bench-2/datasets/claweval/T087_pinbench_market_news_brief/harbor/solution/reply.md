# AI and Cloud Market Brief

**Main topic:** the AI platform market is moving at both ends of the stack at once. Frontier models are getting cheaper and more capable, and the cloud infrastructure under them is being rebuilt around AI workloads.

**Concrete developments**

1. **GPT-5 released** (AI日报, 2026-02-27). OpenAI's GPT-5 adds real-time video input, live translation across 100+ languages, a 40% gain on math and logic benchmarks and better function calling for workflow orchestration. API prices are 30% below GPT-4o.
2. **Kubernetes 1.32 released** (云原生周刊, 2026-02-26). The release targets AI workloads: GPU time-slicing and dynamic allocation, training- and inference-aware scheduling, fast mounting of large datasets and simpler Pod-level seccomp.
3. **RAG keeps evolving** (AI日报, 2026-02-27). Graph RAG, Agentic RAG and multimodal RAG are maturing, with RAGAS-style evaluation becoming standard. The advice is to start with basic RAG and add graph or agentic retrieval as needed.

**Market takeaway for an engineering leader:** capability per dollar is rising fast, so plan for cheaper inference and multimodal inputs rather than locking into today's prices and model choices. If you run GPU workloads on Kubernetes, the 1.32 upgrade is the cheapest utilization win available this quarter. Treat RAG as an evolving platform with its own evaluation, not a one-off integration.
