Pick RSS articles relevant to our AI team and publish a tech newsletter.

## Tools

Each tool is a shell command; `<command> --help` says more.

- `rss list-feeds [--category <string>]`: Get all RSS subscription feeds
- `rss list-articles [--source <string>] [--category <string>] [--max-results <integer>]`: Get a list of articles
- `rss get-article --article-id <string>`: Get the full content of an article
- `rss publish --title <string> --content <string> [--recipients <value> ...]`: Publish a newsletter
